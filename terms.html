Frequently Asked Questions about our company shares and investment guidelines.

1. How many shares are available?
The company consists of a total of 120 shares, and only 60 shares are offered for sale to investors.
2. What are the benefits of being a top shareholder? (Top 3 Managers)
The top 3 shareholders holding the highest number of shares in the company will be granted Manager status, giving them special authority and involvement in the company's strategic decisions.
3. How do dividend payouts work, and are there any fees?
Profits are transferred on a monthly basis. The net profit per share will have a 7% bank transfer fee deducted from it before the final amount is sent to the shareholder.
4. Can I get a refund or return my shares directly to the company?
No, the company does not offer cash refunds or buy back shares directly. Once shares are purchased, they cannot be returned for cash. If you wish to exit the company or liquidate your investment, you can freely sell your shares to other players through the platform's internal shares market.
5. Will I have access to financial reports?
Yes! Every shareholder receives access to a dedicated dashboard to track revenues, expenses, and profits in real time.
6. Can I sell my shares?
Yes, you can freely buy and sell your shares through the platform's internal shares market whenever you want.
7. Are profits guaranteed?
Profits depend entirely on the company's actual market performance and operational success; therefore, returns can vary from month to month.
