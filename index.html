Terms & Conditions / FAQ

1. Total Shares Available

The company consists of a total of 120 shares, and only 60 shares are offered for sale to investors.

2. Management Status (Top 3)

The top 3 shareholders holding the highest number of shares in the company will be granted Manager status, giving them special authority and involvement in the company's strategic decisions.

3. Profit Payouts & Fees

Profits are transferred on a monthly basis. The net profit per share will have a 7% bank transfer fee deducted from it before the final amount is sent to the shareholder.

4. No Cash Refunds

The company does not offer cash refunds or buy back shares directly. Once shares are purchased, they cannot be returned for cash. You can freely sell your shares to other players through the platform's internal shares market.

5. Financial Transparency

Every shareholder receives access to a dedicated dashboard to track revenues, expenses, and profits in real time.

6. Internal Market

Yes, you can freely buy and sell your shares through the platform's internal shares market whenever you want.

7. Market Risk

Profits depend entirely on the company's actual market performance and operational success; therefore, returns can vary from month to month.
